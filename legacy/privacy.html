Privacy Policy

Version 1.0  ·  Last updated —

1. What we collect

In this demo, only the data you enter (name, email, phone, password) is stored in your browser's localStorage/sessionStorage. No data is transmitted to a server.

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to operate your library session (loans, bookings, payments, membership) within this browser.

3. Sharing

No data is shared with third parties in demo mode.

4. Your rights

You may view, export, or clear your demo data from the Superadmin storage panel, or request account deletion via the help page.

5. Cookies

Only essential session cookies/storage are used. No analytics or marketing cookies are active without consent.

6. Security

Demo data is not encrypted at rest and is accessible to anyone with access to your browser. Do not enter real personal data.
