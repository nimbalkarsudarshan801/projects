Common questions

How do I book a seat?

Log in as a student, go to Seat Booking, choose a floor, day, and time slot, then select a free seat and confirm. Payment may be required before the booking is active.

How do I pay a fine?

Go to Fines in your portal, select an unpaid fine, and complete the payment. It stays pending until approved by staff.

Who can approve payments?

Admin and Superadmin roles can review and approve or reject pending payments in the Payments / Payment Approvals section.
